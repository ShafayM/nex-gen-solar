Engineering

Technical excellence, cell to frame.

Every panel in the lineup is batch-tested before it leaves the warehouse and registered against a unique serial number, so what your customer installs is what your customer paid for.

◧

Half-cut cell architecture

Cutting each cell in half lowers resistive loss and improves shade tolerance across all three models.

▦

Multi-busbar design (16 / 18 / 24 BB)

Busbar count scales with the model — NG-585 at 16BB, NG-645 at 18BB, and NG-720 at 24BB — for better micro-crack tolerance and current collection as wattage climbs.

☀

PID & LID resistant

Anti-potential-induced and anti-light-induced degradation cells hold output steady in high-heat, high-humidity grid conditions.

⛶

Bifacial across the lineup

Every model — NG-585, NG-645, and NG-720 — uses dual-glass bifacial construction, harvesting rear-side reflected light for extra yield.

⛈

5400 Pa+ load rating

Reinforced frame profile tested to IEC 61215 / 61730 for heavy wind and hail load.
